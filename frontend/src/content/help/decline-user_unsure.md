**What it means.** The phrasing signalled uncertainty — `maybe around 5000`,
`I am not sure yet`.

**Why it exists separately from [`not provided`](#decline.not_provided).** They
call for different responses. A field with no value needs one. A field the user
is unsure about has a candidate and needs confirmation.

**What to do.** Confirm it plainly when you have decided, and it stores
normally.

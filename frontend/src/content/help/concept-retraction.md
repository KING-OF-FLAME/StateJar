title: Retraction
category: concepts
updated_at: 2026-08-04
summary: Taking a value back, leaving the field genuinely empty.
keywords: retract forget remove delete scratch withdraw
---

**Definition.** Removing a stored value because you said to — `forget the deadline`, `scratch that budget` — leaving the field empty rather than holding a stale value.

**Why it exists.** Without it, the only way to correct a wrong memory is to overwrite it with another wrong value. 'I never said that' has to be expressible.

**Where you see it.** In the `retractions` list, and by the field disappearing from the active state.

**Worked example.** `My deadline is March 3` then `actually forget the deadline`. The deadline field is cleared; the retraction is recorded with what was removed.

**Common misunderstanding.** That a retraction erases the past. It does not — the audit trail still shows the value was once held and then withdrawn. It only stops it being served.

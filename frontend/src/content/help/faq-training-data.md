keywords: training data privacy policy vendor learn

**Not by StateJar.** We do not train on your state, your messages or your
audit trail. There is no training pipeline in this product.

**Your model provider is a separate question** and their policy applies to what
StateJar sends them: your question plus the retrieved subset. Check the policy
of whichever provider you configured — they differ, and some differ by plan.

**If that matters to you, use [Ollama local](#provider.ollama).** Then nothing
reaches any vendor at all; the prompt goes from your browser to your own
daemon.

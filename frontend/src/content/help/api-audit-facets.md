`GET /api/v1/audit/facets`


Powers the filter controls on the [Audit Log screen](#ui.audit_log): which
sessions, providers and models actually appear in your trail, so the filters
offer real options rather than a guess.


Request and response shapes, with copyable samples in four languages, are in the [API Docs](/api-docs) — this entry explains the reasoning, not the schema.

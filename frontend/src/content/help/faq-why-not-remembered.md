Because StateJar refuses to guess, and refusing sometimes means storing
nothing.

Check the declined section first — most of the time there is a row there with a
reason. Walk through
[a value you stated was not stored](#trouble.value-not-stored), which covers all
three cases and what to do about each.

The trade being made is explicit: a system that stores everything it half-
understood will remember more and be wrong more, and it will not tell you
which. In a benefits application or a clinical intake that is the worse
failure. If it is the wrong trade for what you are building, that is a fair
reason not to use StateJar.
